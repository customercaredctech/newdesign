About Admission Navigate

Admission Navigate Consultancy is an educational advisory firm dedicated to helping students find the right fit among Canada's colleges and universities. We believe every student deserves access to world-class education without the stress of navigating complex application processes alone.

We are your partners in:

  • Honest, transparent program and college evaluations
  • Tailored recommendations based on your academic profile and goals
  • Dedicated support through your application journey
  • Clear, judgment-free answers to your questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide immigration advice?
No. Admission Navigate Consultancy is an education consulting firm. We do not have a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC) on staff and do not offer immigration advice. For visa strategy, permanent residency applications, or other regulated immigration matters, we recommend working with a licensed immigration professional.
Do you charge for the initial consultation?
No — your first consultation with us is completely free.
How much does it cost to study in Canada?
Tuition varies by program and institution, typically ranging from $15,000–$35,000 CAD per year. Students should also budget for living expenses, which vary by city and lifestyle — we're happy to walk you through general estimates during your consultation.
What are my career prospects after graduating?
This depends on your program and field of study — many Canadian credentials are well-regarded by employers. We can help you choose programs with strong outcomes, though any questions about work permits or immigration status should go to a licensed immigration professional.
